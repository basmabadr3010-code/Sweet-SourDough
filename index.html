About us

About the bakery

At SWEET & SOUR DOUGH we bake the natural way, 100%. Our sourdough dough ferments slowly and bakes in a stone oven until the crust crackles and the crumb stays soft. Alongside the bread we serve cakes, desserts, savoury sandwiches and drinks — everything prepared fresh every day.

We are in the heart of Marsa Matrouh – Alam El Roum, and our bakers work through the night so you wake up to warm bread. Ordering online is easy: choose, send it on WhatsApp, and we prepare and deliver.
